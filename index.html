How to Use the Value Calculator?

Welcome to our ultimate value calculator! This tool is designed to help players estimate the trading values of their items in real-time. Whether you are looking to trade rare items or want to ensure you are getting a fair deal, our constantly updated database has you covered.

💡 Item Indicators Meaning:

  • 🔥 x/10 : Represents the Demand Rate for the item. The higher the number, the more sought-after it is by the community.
  • ⬆️ : Indicates a Rising Value; the item's current trading worth is steadily increasing.
  • ➡️ : Indicates a Stable Value; the item's worth is currently firm and unchanging.
  • ⬇️ : Indicates a Dropping Value; the item's current trading worth is on a downward trend.
