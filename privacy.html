Privacy Standards & Policy

Effective Period: 2026

At Demon Blade Values Hub, maintaining visitor safety and user transparency is fundamental. This policy outlines our operational practices regarding diagnostic metrics and usage safety across our trading tools.

1. Zero Account Requirements

Our platform operates purely as a public, web-accessible application. We do not prompt user sign-ups, profile creation, or account credentials. We do not ask for or record Roblox logins, user passwords, or confidential inventory credentials.

🔒 Cybersecurity Alert: Never disclose your Roblox authentication tokens or passwords on third-party sites. Our utility functions independently without account bindings.

2. Analytical Insights & Logs

To ensure optimal loading speeds and server stability, we utilize diagnostic software (including Google Analytics). Automated server metrics collect standard non-identifiable parameters:

  • Device browser signatures and operating system metadata.
  • Timestamps of user visits and session durations.
  • Aggregated page interaction counts on value grid components.

3. Advertisements & External Entities

Third-party advertising partners (such as Monetag) may place contextual identifiers (cookies or scripts) to serve relevancy-based communications. These networks function according to their independent privacy documentation.

4. User Acceptance

Interacting with our calculator implies agreement with our privacy protocols and operational standards.
